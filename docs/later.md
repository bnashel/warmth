# Later — Parked Ideas

Things we deliberately deferred. Not commitments; a place so good ideas aren't lost.

- **Rich moment capture** (from Eli's first slider, superseded by the one-screen orb):
  attach a note, a photo, or a song to a feeling; a "recent moments" list.
  If these return, they belong to the private Trail (P4), not the public map.
- **Mood mix** (multi-emotion blend per moment) — the orb commits one hue;
  blends could live in the Trail's reflective view.
- **Own-commit ember** (design-review idea): a transient hot-core glint at
  your own bloom that dissolves into the field — own-device only, so the
  public never-points rule stays intact. The Trail's point renderer is the
  ready-made ingredient.
- **Field scintillation**: density-driven inner sparkle so a busy area reads
  as *many people now*, not just brighter weather.
- **The other shapes of feeling** (bloom / ink / aurora, from Eli's Look
  panel — retired with the panel when the Living Atmosphere landed,
  2026-07-05): the shader knobs still exist (SHAPES in tune.ts). Aurora in
  particular could return as a *clear-cold-night* weather expression.
- **The paper day** (parked 2026-07-06, Eli — constitution rule 1 made
  Warmth always-night): the full 2026-07-02 daylight look — Apple-Maps
  paper, watercolor pigment field (FieldLayer uMode 2), trail stains,
  day road boost/widen — still compiles behind `SOLAR.dayMode` in
  tune.ts, or `?daylight=1` for a no-edit preview. To restore it as the
  default, flip `dayMode: true` and revisit constitution rules 1–3.
